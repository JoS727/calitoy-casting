About Us

About Calitoy Casting

Calitoy Casting is an AI-powered casting studio built for the film and movie industry. We combine generative AI, voice synthesis, and motion capture technology to deliver production-ready digital talent faster and more cost-effectively than traditional casting.

Our Mission

Reimagining Film Casting

We believe every production deserves access to world-class casting, regardless of budget or schedule constraints. Our AI platform democratizes access to photorealistic digital talent, giving independent filmmakers the same casting power as major studios.

🎯

Accessible Casting

High-quality casting capabilities available to productions of every size, from indie shorts to studio blockbusters.

⚡

Speed Without Compromise

Deliver casting in days instead of months, without sacrificing the quality that audiences expect from professional productions.

🧮

Ethical AI

Every voice and likeness is AI-generated or properly licensed. We never clone real people without explicit consent.

Technology

Our Technology Stack

A proprietary pipeline combining state-of-the-art generative models, voice synthesis, and compositing tools.

🧠

Generative Face Models

Custom-trained diffusion models for photorealistic face generation with controllable age, expression, and lighting.

🎤

Voice Synthesis

Neural voice models that produce natural speech with full emotional range, accent control, and real-time generation.

🎥

Motion Synthesis

AI-driven body and facial motion generation for natural movement that matches scene requirements and performance direction.

🎬

Compositing Engine

Automated compositing pipeline that integrates AI talent into live footage with matched lighting, depth, and environmental interaction.

Technologies We Use

Stable Diffusion XL Custom LoRA Models Neural Voice Synthesis Motion Capture AI Real-time Inference 4K Render Pipeline Automated Compositing Expression Transfer Crowd Simulation
