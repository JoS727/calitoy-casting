AI Casting Services

Casting Services

Full spectrum AI-powered casting capabilities for film, television, and digital media productions. Every service is designed to deliver studio-quality results with unprecedented speed.

Digital Talent

Digital Doubles

Create photorealistic digital replicas of actors for stunt work, reshoots, and scenes that would be impossible or dangerous for real performers.

👣

Body Double Generation

Full-body digital doubles with accurate proportions, skin texture, and natural motion. Perfect for stunt sequences and dangerous scenes.

📷

Performance Capture

Transfer real performances onto digital doubles with frame-accurate motion mapping and expression transfer.

🎬

Scene Integration

Seamlessly composite digital doubles into live action footage with matching lighting, depth, and environmental interaction.

Voice

Voice Cloning

Generate authentic, expressive voice models for digital characters. From whisper to shout, every nuance is captured.

🎤

Custom Voice Models

Create entirely new voice profiles with specific age, accent, timbre, and emotional range characteristics.

🎙

Voice Replication

Clone existing voices for reshoots, dubbing, and dialogue replacement without the need for the original actor to re-record.

🎥

Emotional Performance

AI-driven emotional delivery that matches the visual performance, from subtle tension to explosive rage.

Background

Background Talent Generation

Populate your scenes with AI-generated background performers. Thousands of unique individuals, each with distinct appearance and natural movement.

👥

Crowd Generation

Generate crowds of any size with diverse, photorealistic individuals. Control density, diversity, and behavior patterns.

🏠

Period Accuracy

Background talent matched to any era with historically accurate clothing, grooming, and demographics.

🚶

Motion Variety

Each generated individual moves naturally with unique gait, posture, and idle behaviors. No repetition artifacts.

Transformation

De-Aging and Restoration

Make actors younger, older, or transform their appearance with AI-powered age progression and regression.

👦

De-Aging

Reverse the aging process with natural-looking results. Perfect for prequels, flashbacks, and origin stories.

👵

Age Progression

Project actors forward in time for future-set scenes, sequels, and time-skip narratives.

💻

Consistent Results

Frame-by-frame consistency that holds up under scrutiny, even in close-up and high-motion shots.

Replacement

Face Replacement

Swap faces seamlessly between performers or replace existing faces with AI-generated alternatives. Maintains natural expression and lighting.

😎

Face Swap

Replace one face with another while preserving original expressions, head movement, and lighting conditions.

📸

Expression Transfer

Map expressions from one performance onto a different face with sub-millimeter accuracy.

🏁

Lighting Match

Automatically match the replaced face to existing scene lighting, including dynamic and mixed-light environments.

Scale

Crowd Generation

Generate massive crowds and populated scenes without the logistics of real extras. Control every aspect of the crowd from density to individual behavior.

📈

Scalable Crowds

From a dozen to thousands of unique individuals. Each with distinct faces, clothing, and natural movement patterns.

🎳

Behavioral Control

Direct crowd behavior: cheering, panic, casual walking, or standing. Mix behaviors for natural-looking density.

🌁

Environmental Match

Crowds automatically match scene lighting, weather, and atmosphere. No green-screen artifacts or compositing seams.
